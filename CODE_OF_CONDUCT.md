# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We aim for a respectful, accessible, and technically constructive community. Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- welcome different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles;
- give and receive specific, actionable feedback;
- respect privacy, boundaries, attribution, rights, and informed consent; and
- avoid publishing another person's identity, likeness, contact details, credentials, private logs, or source media.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable. Synthetic or generated content must not be used to demean, impersonate, or target people.

## Scope and enforcement

These standards apply in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private channel offered by the hosting platform. Do not include unnecessary personal data or secret material. The owner may warn participants, remove content, limit participation, or ban participants based on severity and context.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a particular resolution timeline. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is unacceptable.
